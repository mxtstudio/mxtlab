Teacher Centre

The MXT Lab Teacher Centre provides professional tools for PNG teachers to plan, deliver and assess curriculum‑aligned lessons.

Key capabilities

  • Curriculum navigation – browse grades, subjects, strands, units, standards, benchmarks and topics.
  • Lesson planner – create lesson plans, teaching notes and classroom activities.
  • Assessment generator – design assignments, quizzes, exams and marking guides.
  • Student analytics – view class performance, learning‑gap identification and teaching priorities.
  • Document centre – download generated lessons, assessments, reports (PDF/DOCX) and analytics.
  • MCP integration – connect Claude, ChatGPT or other AI assistants securely.
  • Subscription management – view plan status and renew.

Get started

Sign in with your Google‑linked MXT Lab account. Teachers will be prompted for verification and subscription status after sign‑in.
