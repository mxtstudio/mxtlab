MXT Lab · PNG Agriculture Education

A dedicated workspace for Agriculture teachers.

Explore teacher access, submit an application, or sign in to open your approved workspace. Student learning remains free and separate from teacher tools.

Get teacher access

Teachers can apply using their MXT Lab account. Applications are reviewed before teacher permissions are granted.

  • Use your own Google-linked MXT Lab account.
  • Submit your school and teaching details with supporting documents.
  • Wait for an administrator to review and approve your application.
Apply for Teacher Access Open Teacher Workspace

Sign in to check your teacher access. Approved staff must also be assigned to a department to use Teacher OS.

What is available now?

The current Teacher OS supports department-based lesson planning, submission and review for approved teachers and department administrators. Other teacher tools and integrations are still being developed; they are not presented as active features until they are ready.
