Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

MXT Lab is an independent Agriculture learning platform for Grade 9–12 students and teachers in Papua New Guinea. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, who may process it, and how you can ask us to access, correct or delete it.

1. Who operates MXT Lab

MXT Lab is operated from Papua New Guinea. For privacy questions or requests, contact mxtlab.png@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: when you sign in with Google, we receive account identifiers and profile information made available by Google, such as your name, email address and profile image. MXT Lab also stores your account role and relevant school/profile details.
  • Learning records: lesson progress, quiz attempts, answers, scores, completion status and related learning activity saved to your account.
  • Teacher applications: applicant name, email, phone number, school, province, district, position, subjects, teaching experience, application status, review notes and supporting PDF/JPG/PNG documents you upload.
  • Teacher/student relationships: information needed to link a student to an authorised teacher or school and to show learning progress to that teacher.
  • Messages: details and message content you submit through our contact form, which is processed by Formspree.
  • Technical and security information: browser/device information, approximate location derived by service providers, IP address, timestamps, diagnostic events and security logs needed to operate and protect the platform.
  • Usage and advertising information: pages visited, interactions, cookie or similar identifiers, and information collected by analytics or advertising partners when their scripts are active.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide lessons and assessments, save learning history, show students their own progress, let authorised teachers review linked students' progress, assess teacher applications, respond to enquiries, maintain platform reliability, prevent abuse, understand site usage and support the platform through advertising. We do not sell personal information.

4. Student records and teacher access

Learning records are associated with the student's account so that progress can remain with the student if their school or teacher changes. A teacher should only be able to view progress for students linked to that teacher through the platform's authorised relationship controls. Teacher applications and their supporting documents are private and are available only to the applicant and authorised administrators. Do not upload identity documents, medical information or other sensitive material unless MXT Lab specifically asks for it.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

MXT Lab uses browser storage to remember preferences such as the display theme and to support sign-in sessions. Google Analytics may collect usage and device information to help us understand how the site is used. The site also displays advertising served by Adsterra; advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to measure ads and, where enabled by their settings, personalise advertising. These partners process information under their own privacy policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. For Google advertising controls, visit Google My Ad Center. Adsterra advertising preferences are managed through the relevant provider and your browser controls.

6. Service providers and international processing

We use service providers to operate MXT Lab, including Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Vercel for hosting and deployment; Google for sign-in, analytics and fonts; Formspree for contact-form submissions; and Adsterra for advertising. Our Supabase project is hosted in Singapore. These providers may process information in countries other than Papua New Guinea and are subject to their own security and privacy terms. We share only information needed for the relevant service.

7. Retention and deletion requests

We retain account and learning records while they are needed to provide the service and preserve learning history. Teacher application documents and review records are retained for application administration, security and legitimate recordkeeping. Technical logs are retained for operational and security purposes. We do not yet publish a fixed retention period for every category of information. To request access, correction or deletion, email mxtlab.png@gmail.com from the account address and identify the information involved. We may need to verify your identity. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

8. Security

We use access controls, database row-level security, private storage for teacher application documents and other reasonable technical measures to protect information. No website or transmission method is completely secure. Please keep your Google account secure, sign out on shared devices and do not share passwords or access links.

9. Children and school-age users

MXT Lab is designed for secondary-school learners and teachers. Students should use the platform with appropriate parent, guardian or school guidance. The platform is not intended for children under 13 to create accounts independently. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required permission, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your choices and contact

You may choose not to sign in, although account-only features such as saved progress will then be unavailable. You can manage browser cookies, sign out of your account, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Questions or concerns: mxtlab.png@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the platform or its service providers change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
